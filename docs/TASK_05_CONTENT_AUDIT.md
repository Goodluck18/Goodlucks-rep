# Task 05 — Correcting misleading information and improving trust content

Status: implemented on `arena/c3016349-goodlucks-rep`, based on `main` @ `bb897df`
(Task 04 merge). Wording only: no routes, navigation, dashboard structure, business
rule, fee, commission or schema change.

The platform's role, stated once and used consistently:

> Goodlucks Homes is a property discovery and rental intermediary. Landlords and
> agents submit listings; Goodlucks reviews each listing, publishes it, takes the
> enquiries and viewing requests, and keeps the record. Goodlucks does not own,
> manage or physically inspect the properties, and the tenancy agreement is
> completed directly between the tenant and the landlord or agent.

---

## 2. What the copy says now

* Homepage hero: “Goodlucks Homes connects people looking for a home with the landlords and agents who list them. Browse the catalogue, request a viewing, and agree a time with the landlord or agent. Your tenancy agreement is completed directly with them.”
* Section label: “Submitted by registered landlords and agents”; heading “Featured properties”.
* “How it works” step 2: “Request a viewing on any listing. The request goes to the landlord or agent responsible for it, and they contact you to agree a time. Nothing is confirmed until you have both agreed.”
* Step 3: “After the viewing, any viewing fee is paid as the listing states — never online. Your tenancy agreement is completed directly with the landlord or agent, outside the platform.”
* Trust cards: “Staff-reviewed listings” (with the explicit limit: “not an ownership check, an inspection of the property or a guarantee of its condition”), “One place to sort it out”, and “Viewing guidance” — the balanced advice the brief asked for (agree the time before travelling, meet the landlord or agent, view before committing, pay as the terms state, never online).
* Footer: “…We do not own or manage the properties listed here.”
* About: rewritten mission, FAQ 1 (what Goodlucks does), FAQ 2 (how matching works, “a match is a suggestion, not a reservation”), FAQ 4 (review is not an inspection and not proof of ownership), FAQ 5 (why enquiries go through the platform, with no fraud rhetoric and no contact-release promise), FAQ 6 (modest coverage claim). The three pillars are now “Listings reviewed”, “Viewings arranged”, “Where we operate”.
* Property card: “Reviewed by Goodlucks” when staff have reviewed the listing, and **no badge at all** otherwise.
* Detail page: “Arrange Your Viewing Through Goodlucks”, plus the practical guidance bullet. The existing honest line “Listing details reviewed by our team (not an ownership check)” is unchanged.
* Verification: “Your documents are used only to check your identity and to administer your account. They are stored privately and are never shown on your public profile or on a listing. Only you and authorised reviewers can open them, and every time a document is opened it is recorded.” — followed by the encryption state (worded without naming keys or environment) and the retention window.
* Emails/notifications: “review” instead of “verify”, “live listings” instead of “verified listings”, viewings arranged with the landlord or agent.

## 3. Security and privacy: what is claimed, and what supports it

Every statement in the new verification note maps to code:

| Claim | Support |
| --- | --- |
| Used only for identity checks and account administration | `submit_identity_verification()` / `submit_professional_authorisation()` in `php-backend/lib/verification.php` |
| Stored privately, not publicly served | `php-backend/lib/private_storage.php`: private directory outside `uploads/`, mode 0700, deny-all `.htaccess`, API root refuses `storage/` |
| Only the owner and authorised reviewers can open them | `user_can_read_private_document()` + `can_access_identity_documents` capability (granted from the server console only) |
| Every open is recorded | `audit_log('document.viewed', …)` in `serve_private_document()` |
| Identity number encrypted *when the key is configured*, otherwise not retained | `encrypt_sensitive()` / `identity_data_key()` (`encryptionActive`); the number is dropped when no key exists |
| Deleted after the retention window | `purge_verification_personal_data()` + `retention_purge_after`, run by `php-backend/cron.php` |

Not claimed anywhere: document-file encryption at rest, third-party sharing guarantees, or a retention period beyond the configured window. The uploaded **document files are not encrypted at rest** — they are protected by private storage and access control. That is a genuine limitation, not a wording problem, and is recorded below rather than papered over.

Ghana Card numbers/documents are never included in public property payloads, profile payloads, notifications or emails (checked in `repository.php`, `auth.php`, `verification.php`).

## 4. Financial wording (unchanged rules, corrected text)

No fee, percentage or policy was altered. The 8% landlord platform fee on rent, the admin-set general landlord viewing fee, the agent's own per-property fee and the 60%/70% agent tiers are untouched (`php-backend/lib/landlord_policy.php`, `finance.php`, `verification.php`).

Two pieces of copy were corrected so they no longer contradict those rules:

* the viewing-request confirmation now shows *this* property's fee: a platform-policy listing says “payable after the viewing, to Goodlucks Homes”, an agent listing says “paid directly to the agent on site”;
* the viewing-update email no longer says every fee is “paid on site”, and now says the listing states who it is paid to.

The About FAQ keeps the Task 03 distinction (agent sets their own fee; Goodlucks sets one general landlord fee, paid to Goodlucks after the viewing, not to the landlord).
